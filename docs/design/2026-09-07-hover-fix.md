# Notch hover stability

The hover preview lowered itself to the floating window level, beneath the menu bar, on expansion. Its tracking area was also replaced on every resize with an assumed-inside state, and raw exit events immediately requested collapse. A global-only mouse monitor missed events delivered to Topsoil itself and was removed during collapse grace.

The fix keeps the collapsed notch and compact preview above the menu bar, retains one tracking area synchronized to visible bounds, uses actual screen-space pointer containment for tracking events, and installs complementary local/global mouse-move monitors while shown. Re-entry remains observable during the grace period and the grace deadline rechecks pointer containment. Click-opened workspaces retain their floating level for drag sources.

Validation: the new regression cases first produced five failures, then passed. Final suite: 428 checks, zero failures. With user authorization, a macOS pointer sweep was run against the old and fixed packaged builds, sampling the live panel height every 100ms for 1.2 seconds at each position.

- Before: left and right notch edge entries intermittently changed from 429pt to 32pt and back; moving into the preview body left it collapsed at 32pt.
- After: center, both edges, and preview body remained at 429pt for all samples. Outside movement collapsed normally to 32pt after the grace period.

The new build was packaged and launched locally. Regression coverage includes hover window level, stale exit events at multiple interior positions, missed re-entry during grace, and tracking-area identity across resize.
